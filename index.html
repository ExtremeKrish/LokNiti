Understanding the Axes

How we map the intricate alignments of the Indian subcontinent

Horizontal Axis: Economic & Cultural Alignment

Historically, India's economic debate centers on the degree of state intervention versus private enterprise. The Left favors extensive welfare networks, protectionist labor laws, state public sectors, and progressive social models. The Right advocates for privatization, deregulation, ease of business, and is traditionally coupled with preserving cultural history and identity integration.

LEFT (Socialist / State welfare) RIGHT (Capitalist / Nationalist)

Vertical Axis: Governance & Civil Freedom

Focuses on state authority versus individual liberty. Authoritarians believe the government should exercise strict order to protect unity, manage public morals, and preserve security against external or internal divides. Libertarians argue for decentralized state powers, free speech absolute rights, privacy, and full autonomy over personal lifestyle or food preferences.

LIBERTARIAN (Freedom / Decentralized) AUTHORITARIAN (Order / Centralized)

About LokNiti

LokNiti India is an educational civic visualization web app designed to map political opinions relative to the multifaceted public debates of modern India.

Unlike Western compasses that focus strictly on private taxation versus social liberalism, this model factors in major Indian policies like agrarian state support (MSP), social representation programs (reservations), cultural codification laws (UCC), and internal federal systems.

All scores are normalized dynamic coordinates based on balanced weighted indicators. No data is stored, shared, or compiled outside your browser context.
